WELCOME TO MY PORTFOLIO

Hi! I'm Viola, and I love making things.

For me painting is a way of observing the world: I mainly focus on the human figure, and with my drawings and paintings I try to capture a person's energy.

I study painting at the Academy of Fine Arts, a path that has allowed me to deepen my research on body's expressive possibilities.

Alongside my artistic practice, I worked for two years as a sewing assistant, where I refined my knowledge of garment-making and my manual skills.
